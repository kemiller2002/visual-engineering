## Original requirements and acceptance criteria from GitHub issue #49

## Background
For Summa, unsupported Forma tokens fall back to hard-coded blue/red values. Signal consumes Forma but does not explicitly select its product palette in static page markup. Existing behavioral, layout and accessibility checks do not establish sound visual hierarchy or coherent product identity.

## Requirements
- **VE-COLOR-001.1:** Publish a normative product visual-decision standard distinguishing brand, theme, semantic status, task hierarchy, aesthetic fit, contrast, and non-color meaning. Reuse existing theme research and UI decision checklists rather than replacing them.
- **VE-COLOR-001.2:** Require a reviewable product palette/hierarchy rationale *before* implementation or a clearly identified provisional state; no agent-invented approvals. Specify when a human/design review is necessary versus a repeatable policy choice.
- **VE-COLOR-001.3:** Define representative browser evidence matrix: critical screens/states, light/dark, desktop/mobile (including 320/390/768/1280 px as practical), 200% zoom, focus, success/empty/error/unknown states, forced colors, grayscale and CVD screening. Rendering is evidence, not a substitute for an independent evaluator.
- **VE-COLOR-001.4:** Provide a standardized review record with source commit, Forma version, selected theme, requirement/decision IDs, computed colors/contrast, screenshots, reviewer observations, unresolved unknowns and disposition.
- **VE-COLOR-001.5:** Define *separate* pass/fail dimensions: machine-valid token usage; accessibility/semantic integrity; functional visual hierarchy and coherence. Contrast PASS cannot automatically imply design PASS.
- **VE-COLOR-001.6:** Maintain an adoption matrix and rollout checklist for Summa and Signal first, then other maintained Echelon apps; track genuine exceptions and remove repeated defect classes upstream.

## Acceptance criteria
- An agent can consume the standard and produce an unambiguous visual decision record and evidence manifest for an application.
- Reviewers can identify the intent, what was tested, what remains unknown and why the design was accepted; absence of screenshots or test coverage is UNKNOWN, never PASS.
- Pilot with Summa and Signal, including at least one intentionally wrong-but-high-contrast palette and one low-contrast dark-theme case.
- Cross-link existing [universal UI conformance rollout #47](https://github.com/kemiller2002/visual-engineering/issues/47), [theme specimen catalog #11](https://github.com/kemiller2002/visual-engineering/issues/11), [perceptual theme research #13](https://github.com/kemiller2002/visual-engineering/issues/13), and [controlled variants #14](https://github.com/kemiller2002/visual-engineering/issues/14).

## Scope
Visual Engineering owns normative standard and evaluation criteria; Ordo owns approved decision IR, Forma owns public styling contract, Dokimos owns independent static checks, Praxis owns execution gates.
