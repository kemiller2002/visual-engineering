## Rules

- A disability diagnosis may be used for recruitment, stratification, and subgroup analysis.
- A diagnosis must not be treated as proof of the active perceptual bottleneck.
- Preference and performance outcomes must be recorded separately.
- Simulation results must be labeled as simulation.
- Null and adverse results must remain in the registry.
- A hypothesis is not promoted to a principle until replicated or supported by converging evidence.
