# Perceptual Envelope Evidence Registry

## EV-VE-PE-001

**Source:** Azzarello C, Paek S, Hodge C, Jameson M, Lewis J. *Does font improve reading in dyslexic children?: meta-analysis of dyslexia-friendly fonts and dyslexic children's reading performance.* Annals of Dyslexia. 2026. PMID: 42536336.

**Finding used:** Across 15 empirical studies, 91 effect sizes, and 688 dyslexic readers, dyslexia-oriented fonts did not produce a consistent or reliable improvement in reading speed or accuracy.

**Supports:** rejection of universal diagnosis-to-font mapping.

**Does not prove:** typography is irrelevant to dyslexic readers.

## EV-VE-PE-002

**Source:** Wallace S et al. *Towards Individuated Reading Experiences: Different Fonts Increase Reading Speed for Different Individuals.* ACM TOCHI. 2022.

**Finding used:** Within-reader reading speed varied substantially across fonts without a comprehension penalty; the fastest font differed by individual.

**Supports:** individualized typography research and preference/performance separation.

## EV-VE-PE-003

**Source:** *Optimizing text for an individual's visual system: The contribution of visual crowding to reading difficulties.* Cortex. 2018.

**Finding used:** A subgroup of participants with dyslexia and elevated visual crowding read faster with increased letter, word, and line spacing.

**Supports:** mechanism-based intervention.

**Does not prove:** crowding explains dyslexia generally.

## EV-VE-PE-004

**Source:** REP-VE-COL-001 in this repository.

**Finding used:** low vision and CVD require a layered visibility, discrimination, redundancy, adaptability, and task-validation model; simulations are screens rather than proof.

## EV-VE-PE-005

**Source:** W3C Accessibility Guidelines 3.0 Working Draft, 10 September 2026.

**Finding used:** the WCAG 3 text contrast algorithm remains undetermined; the draft expects size/weight to matter and notes a possible separate red/green CVD requirement.

**Use restriction:** WCAG 3 is a Working Draft. WCAG 2.2 remains the current conformance baseline.

## EV-VE-PE-006

**Source:** Cai T et al. *Personalized Font Recommendations: Combining ML and Typographic Guidelines to Optimize Readability.* DIS 2022.

**Finding used:** participant and font characteristics can support individualized font ranking with reading-speed gains relative to fixed defaults.

## EV-VE-PE-007

**Source:** Project Atlas Typography Autonomous Research Report v1.

**Finding used:** typography variables have conditional optima; active bottlenecks, task-weighted error, familiarity, and compensation cost must be modeled separately.
