# Project Atlas - Masters vs. Modern Science 02: Josef Albers

## Executive Summary

Josef Albers transformed contextual color effects into a repeatable educational methodology. His central idea—that color is perceived relationally rather than absolutely—is strongly supported by modern color appearance science.

## Core Claims and Verdicts

### Color is relative
Verdict: Strongly supported.

### Identical colors can appear different
Verdict: Strongly supported through simultaneous contrast and contextual appearance research.

### Different colors can appear identical
Verdict: Supported through metamerism and contextual appearance.

### Observation is essential
Verdict: Supported for perceptual training, complemented by quantitative models.

## What Albers Got Right

- Context dominates isolated swatches.
- Perception should be trained.
- Color interaction is more important than memorizing palettes.
- Practical experiments reveal hidden assumptions.

## What Modern Science Adds

- Chromatic adaptation
- Simultaneous contrast
- Assimilation
- Opponent processing
- Color appearance models
- Perceptual color spaces
